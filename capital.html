Doctrine for the Capital

  • Every level is Vigor now. Dex and End are finished at 40/20 (base). 50 Vigor by Morgott's fog — that's the whole spending plan.
  • Soreseal check-in: this is the promised re-evaluation point. Keep it through the city; if Morgott specifically kills you in bursts that feel unfair, swap it for Crimson Amber's bigger sibling or a defensive pick for that fight only.
  • Buff-then-summon: the Mimic copies active buffs at the moment of summoning. Fowl Foot, Physick, anything — apply first, summon second.
  • The Avatar rematch is optional forever. Dragonbarrow's rot golem still holds your crystal tears. At 75+ with boluses it's a 90-second errand whenever you feel like collecting.
  • Rune Arcs flow now. Leyndell's rats and loot drop them steadily — you can afford one per serious boss attempt with Radahn's rune (+15% HP/FP/stamina).
  • After Morgott: the original route completes. The next map (Mountaintops, and the roads beyond) gets drawn when the king is dead — one campaign at a time.
