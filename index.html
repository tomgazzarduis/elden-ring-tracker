The Runebook

Level is derived from the eight stats, not stored — so it can never drift out of sync again. Red labels are values the sync could not confirm; correct them and they turn gold.
Vigor 60
Hit points. Soft caps at 40 and again at 60 — you are exactly on the second cap, where each further point is worth roughly 6 HP. Hard stop. Do not spend here again.
Mind 11
FP — and your build's actual constraint. Unsheathe costs FP every use, and 11 Mind buys very few casts per blue flask. This is now the top of the spending queue, above Endurance and Dex. Priority 1 · take it to 25
Endurance 40
Stamina and equip load. Governs how long an L1 chain can run before you are stranded mid-combo, and whether you still medium-roll in your current armour. Priority 2 · 40 → 50
Dexterity 60
Scales Keen weapons. The old plan stopped at 40; you have since pushed past it. The next meaningful breakpoint is 80, after which it is genuinely finished. Priority 3 · 60 → 80
Strength 12
Samurai class floor, and correctly untouched. Keen affinity scales Strength at close to zero. An earlier note claimed the Samurai floor was 15 — that was wrong; it is 12 STR and 15 DEX. Never spend here
Intelligence 9
Class floor. The legendary sorceries only need to be acquired, never cast, so the collection trophy costs you nothing here. Never spend here
Faith 8
Class floor. Same logic as Intelligence — the legendary incantations count as owned the moment they enter your inventory. Never spend here
Arcane 8
Class floor. Arcane raises bleed build-up, which is tempting on a dual-katana build — but Keen scaling is Dex-driven and a respec toward Arcane would cost more than it returns this late. Not worth switching to now
Main hand +25
Maxed. Keen Uchigatana +25, carrying Unsheathe. God-Slaying Armament already popped. Note both katanas are regular-path weapons — the banked Ancient Dragon Somber stone cannot touch them. Finished · save the Somber stone for Hand of Malenia
Offhand +25
Also maxed, carrying Bloody Slash mostly to hold the Keen affinity. Powerstance damage comes off both blades, so having them level is the whole point. Finished
Mimic Tear +10
Maximum. It copies your gear and stats at the moment of summoning, so it is now a level-129 samurai with two +25 katanas. Buff first, summon second — it copies active buffs too. Finished · matters most against Malenia
Flask charges 10
Not confirmed by the sync — last recorded as ~10 back in July, and you have crossed two regions of Golden Seeds since. Flask count is the single largest lever still available for Malenia. Check at a grace · 12–14 before Elphael

The Remaining Route

Ordered so the cheap collection-closers come first and the skill wall comes last. Every objective shows what it costs in minutes and what finishing it unlocks — because a thirty-minute puzzle that opens four hours of content should not sort next to a thirty-minute errand that opens nothing.
01 · CONSECRATED SNOWFIELD~60 min
The Golden Order Greatsword
Gate: already open — both Haligtree Medallion halves are held. Hold the medallion at the Grand Lift of Rold rather than pressing the obvious prompt.
Unlocks: closes Legendary Armaments outright — 9 of 9. One trophy from one pickup.
The snowstorm kills navigation. Ride the left-hand cliff edge and you cannot get lost.
02 · SELLIA HIDEAWAY, CAELID~30 min
Stars of Ruin
Gate: needs the Sellian Sealbreaker from Sellen's questline. Check your Key Items tab before travelling — without it the cell will not open and the trip is wasted.
Unlocks: one of the two remaining legendary spells.
The hideaway is the illusory-wall cave behind Sellia. Hit walls that look wrong.
03 · DEEPROOT DEPTHS~20 min
Elden Stars — verify first
Conflict in the record: the ledger lists this as missing, but the 13 August session logged picking it up in Deeproot. Check your inventory before you travel — if it is held, this objective vanishes and the spell category becomes 6 of 7.
Unlocks: with Stars of Ruin, closes Legendary Sorceries and Incantations.
04 · SAINTED HERO'S GRAVE, ALTUS~30 min
Ancient Dragon Knight Kristoff
Unlocks: the fifth of six legendary ashes. Only Cleanrot Knight Finlay in Elphael remains after this.
Chariot dungeon. The trick is the side alcoves and the two braziers, not out-running anything.
05 · MOHGWYN PALACE~75 min
Mohg, Lord of Blood
Gate: the Purifying Crystal Tear. In your physick it cancels the Nihil ritual outright; without it, Nihil is three near-death events you must heal through. Sync could not confirm you have it.
Unlocks: Shardbearer Mohg, plus his Great Rune. Also the entry requirement for Shadow of the Erdtree, alongside Radahn.
The swamp's sleeping birds are the best rune farm in the game if Mind and Endurance need funding.
06 · ORDINA, LITURGICAL TOWN~30 min
The Evergaol Puzzle
Unlocks: roughly four hours of content. This is the highest-leverage half hour left on the board — it is the only door to the Haligtree, Elphael, Loretta, Malenia, Finlay, Marika's Soreseal and the Dragoncrest Greatshield.
Sneak, do not fight. Use Sentry's Torch or throw pots to reveal them; the northeast seal is the one that kills people.
07 · MIQUELLA'S HALIGTREE~90 min
Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree
The branch-walking above the void is more dangerous than anything in the area. The Erdtree Avatar on the approach is optional.
08 · ELPHAEL, BRACE OF THE HALIGTREE~2 hrs
Two Categories Close Here
Unlocks: closes Legendary Talismans and Legendary Ashen Remains in a single area. Two trophies.
Two hours of scarlet rot. Boluses are effectively craft-only — count them before you go in. Going in short turns this into a three-hour area.
09 · MALENIA, BLADE OF MIQUELLAopen-ended
The Only Genuine Wall
Not solvable by level. Everything else on this list bends to being over-levelled; she does not. Waterfowl Dance is the exam and the answer is spacing, not rolling.
She heals on every hit she lands, including hits on your Mimic. A blocked hit still heals her. This is why the fight punishes passivity.
Pre-buff, then summon: your +10 Mimic arrives as a level-129 samurai with two +25 katanas and takes roughly half her attention.
Bleed procs are your best damage. Powerstance L1 chains build it fastest — the same rotation that has worked all game.
10 · LEYNDELL, ASHEN CAPITAL~45 min
Hoarah Loux, Warrior
Sir Gideon Ofnir stands before him and carries no trophy — he casts the spells of every boss you have killed, which by now is a long list.
Do this after Malenia. Nothing here is missable and there is no reason to be closer to the ending than you must be.
11 · THE ENDINGS~2 hrs
The Save-File Protocol
Auto-sync stays off until all three have popped. It will silently overwrite the backup you are depending on, and that is the single most common way this protocol fails.
Frenzied Flame goes first deliberately: it is the ending that locks the others, so it is taken when a restore is guaranteed to follow. New Game Plus has no bearing on the platinum.

What Can Still Go Wrong

  • Fighting the Elden Beast before the other 39 have popped. The endings protocol assumes everything else is banked. Check the trophy list, not your memory.
  • PS5 auto-sync during the ending juggle. It overwrites the backup silently. Off before the first ending, on only after the third.
  • Travelling to Sellia without the Sealbreaker, or to Deeproot for a spell you already own. Both are in the five-minute check above.
  • Mohg without the Purifying Tear. Not fatal, but it converts a formality into three near-death events mid-fight.
  • Elphael on a short bolus count. Two hours of scarlet rot with nothing to cure it becomes three hours and a lot of running back.
  • Trophies failing to pop offline. Some players report this. If you play disconnected, connect once before the ending sequence.

Doctrine, Final Revision

  • Mind is now the build's constraint. "Mind untouched by design" was right at Mind 11 with no FP costs. Unsheathe is your punish and it is FP-gated — 25 Mind is the highest-value spend left.
  • Vigor is finished at 60. Roughly 6 HP per level past the cap. Nothing to gain.
  • Never store what you can derive. Level drifted from 126 to 129 across two documents because it was recorded rather than calculated. It is computed here.
  • Unlocks matter more than duration. Ordina is thirty minutes and opens four hours. Sort by what a task frees, not what it costs.
  • Malenia is not a levelling problem. Every other wall in this game bent to being over-levelled. She will not. Go in expecting to learn her.
  • Rule 1 survives to the end. Never farm as a standalone activity — and Mohgwyn's birds are on ground you are crossing anyway, so they do not count.
  • Verify before travelling. Rule 8 has now caught a wrong Samurai stat floor, a drifted level, and two collection lists I had wrong. Keep doing it.
