11 · THE ENDINGS~2 hrs
The Save-File Protocol
Read this before you go in: Radagon and the Elden Beast are immune to bleed — and to every other status effect. Your entire damage model switches off for the last fight, and you will fight it three times. Plan on raw physical damage, Unsheathe burst, and patience.
Auto-sync stays off until all three pop. It overwrites the backup silently, and that is the usual way this protocol fails.
Frenzied Flame goes first deliberately: it is the ending that locks the others, so take it when a restore is guaranteed to follow. New Game Plus has no bearing on the platinum.

The uncomfortable finding

You are collecting nine legendary armaments for a trophy and, at STR 12 / FAI 8 / INT 9 / ARC 8, you cannot currently wield a single one of them. That is fine — the trophy only requires acquiring them. But two are within a handful of points, and one of those is the weapon the sync already earmarked your banked Ancient Dragon stone for.

Three cheap unlocks, ranked
  • +2 Faith → Eclipse Shotel. Two levels. You already own it, and it is currently dead weight in your box. The cheapest new toy available.
  • +4 Strength → Hand of Malenia. Needs 16 STR / 48 DEX; your Dex is already 60. It drops from the boss you are building toward, and the Ancient Dragon Somber stone you banked exists precisely to max it. This contradicts the standing "never spend on Strength" rule — the rule was written when nothing you wanted needed Strength. Four points is not a build change, it is a key.
  • +12 Arcane → Rivers of Blood. 12 STR / 18 DEX / 20 ARC. This one is a Larval Tear respec rather than a few levels, and Arcane also raises your bleed build-up across the board. The most powerful option and the most disruptive.
Bleed, boss by boss

Mohg, Lord of Blood

Bleed at 50%

Takes half the usual hemorrhage damage — thematically fair for the blood lord. Still worth proccing, just slower.

Use: your current katanas. He is weak to plain physical. Purifying Tear for Nihil.

Loretta, Knight of the Haligtree

Bleeds normally

No resistance worth planning around. A mounted caster who wants distance.

Use: standard powerstance rotation, closed distance.

Malenia, Blade of Miquella

Low resistance — your best matchup

One of the easiest bosses in the game to bleed. Procs land fast enough to outpace her lifesteal, which is the whole problem with her otherwise.

Use: dual Uchigatana L1 chains. This is the fight your build was accidentally designed for.

Radagon & the Elden Beast

Immune — to bleed and every status

The final fight switches your damage model off entirely, and you will run it three times for three endings.

Use: raw physical and Unsheathe burst. Radagon has no fire resistance if you fancy a Physick tear. The Elden Beast resists holy heavily.

What can still go wrong

6
  • Fighting the Elden Beast before the other 39 have popped. The protocol assumes everything else is banked. Check the list, not your memory.
  • PS5 auto-sync during the ending juggle. It overwrites the backup silently. Off before the first ending, on after the third.
  • Travelling to Sellia without the Sealbreaker, or to Deeproot for a spell you already own.
  • Mohg without the Purifying Tear. Not fatal, but it turns a formality into three near-death events.
  • Elphael on a short bolus count. Two hours of scarlet rot with no cure becomes three hours and a lot of running back.
  • Bringing a bleed mindset to Radagon. He and the Elden Beast are immune to every status in the game.

Doctrine, final revision

7
  • Mind is now the constraint. "Mind untouched by design" was right at zero FP costs. Unsheathe is your punish and it is FP-gated — 25 Mind is the best spend left.
  • Vigor is finished at 60. Roughly 6 HP per level past the cap.
  • Never store what you can derive. Level drifted 126 vs 129 across two documents because it was recorded rather than calculated.
  • Unlocks matter more than duration. Ordina is thirty minutes and opens four hours.
  • Malenia is not a levelling problem — but she is a bleed problem, and you are holding the answer.
  • The Strength rule has an exception now. It existed because nothing you wanted needed Strength. Hand of Malenia needs four points. Rules serve the build, not the reverse.
  • Verify before travelling. Rule 8 has now caught a wrong Samurai stat floor, a drifted level, and two collection lists I had wrong.
