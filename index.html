Your second katana's upgrade level. Powerstancing swings both weapons, so every L1 chain deals damage from each one — an offhand lagging several levels behind is a quiet, permanent DPS loss. Keep it within ~2 levels of the main hand
Vigor 49
Hit points — the most valuable stat in the game for nearly every build. It soft caps at 40 and again at 60; points past 60 barely register on the bar. 50 before Morgott · 60 before the Haligtree
Endurance 20
Stamina (attacks, rolls, sprinting) and equip load (how heavy you can dress while still medium-rolling). For dual katanas it's the stat that decides whether you can finish an L1 chain and still dodge out of it. 25–30 is plenty — more only if you wear heavy armour
Dexterity 40
Scales Keen weapons — your entire damage model. Returns fall off a cliff at 40, which is why the plan called it finished forever. Anything above 40 is runes that should have gone to Vigor. 40 and done · Rennala refunds overspend for a Larval Tear
Mind 11
FP, which fuels Ashes of War. Matters more to you than it looks — Unsheathe costs FP every use. At 11 you get very few casts per blue flask. Your Mimic costs HP, not FP, so it's unaffected either way. 15–20 if you ever run dry mid-boss · otherwise ignore
Flask charges 10
Total Crimson + Cerulean, raised by Golden Seeds at any grace. Usually a bigger survivability gain than more Vigor — it's more total healing rather than a bigger bar. Sacred Tears make each sip heal harder. 12–14 before the Haligtree
Mimic Tear +4
Your spirit summon's upgrade level, fed Ghost Glovewort to Roderika. It also copies your current gear and stats, so every weapon upgrade strengthens it for free. +8 before Act III · Glovewort [5]+ litters Ainsel & Deeproot

Read this before the Forge: burning the Erdtree is the game's real point of no return. Leyndell's NPCs, its merchants, and several quest steps are gone the moment you light it. Everything in Acts I–III should be finished first. That is the entire reason this guide is ordered the way it is.
ACT V · THREE ENDINGS, ONE RUN · ~2-3 hrs
The Save-File Gambit
START: the last grace before Radagon. DO: back up your save, then collect all three endings by reloading. DONE WHEN: the platinum pings.
The needle is your insurance. If you'd rather not juggle save files at all, Miquella's Needle lets you take Frenzied Flame, cure it at Placidusax's arena, and continue to another ending in the same save. Slower, but nothing can go wrong with it.
The one true trap: accepting the Frenzied Flame without either a backup or the needle permanently locks Age of the Stars and Elden Lord for that save. This is the single most common platinum-killer in the game.

Things That Permanently Ruin a Platinum Run

  • Burning the Erdtree too early. Leyndell's NPCs and several quest steps die with the city. Acts I–III first, always.
  • Taking the Frenzied Flame with no exit. Without a save backup or Miquella's Needle, two of the three ending trophies are gone for that save file.
  • Killing quest NPCs for their gear. Every one of them is worth more alive. Nothing you get from a corpse beats a finished questline.
  • Ranni before Age of the Stars. The ending is quest-gated, not choice-gated. You hold the Fingerslayer Blade — deliver it.
  • Millicent's chain started too late. It begins in Caelid and ends in Elphael. Start it in Act II or you'll be crossing the world again in Act III.
  • PS5 cloud auto-upload during the ending juggle. It will silently overwrite the backup you're depending on. Switch it off before Act V.

Amended Doctrine for the Platinum

  • Rule 7 is suspended. "Sprawl gets bookmarked, not explored" won the first clear and would cost you the platinum. Act II is the sprawl, collected deliberately.
  • Rule 2 still holds absolutely. Weapon level > character level; flasks > both. A +25 katana at level 120 beats a +20 at 150.
  • Vigor to 60, then stop. Diminishing returns bite hard past 60. Everything after goes to Endurance, then wherever you like.
  • The Mimic scales with you. Every weapon upgrade and every level makes it stronger for free. Feed it Glovewort while you're underground in Act II.
  • Buff first, summon second. Unchanged, and it matters most against Malenia.
  • Two Great Runes minimum. Morgott's is a straight upgrade over Radahn's for boss attempts once you have it.
  • Cross-reference before travel. Rule 8 earned its place — quest triggers are the most patch-sensitive thing in this game.
  • Malenia last. Every level and every upgrade helps. There is no prize for meeting her early.
